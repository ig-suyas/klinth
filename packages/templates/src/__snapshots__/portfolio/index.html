About

Hello, I am Ira.

I have spent nine years designing for studios, start-ups and nonprofits. These days I work alone, which means you talk to the person doing the work, every step of the way.

I care about clear words, generous space and details that make people feel looked after. Outside work I draw maps, bake bread and walk too far.
