Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

The short version
  • There are no accounts. We do not know who you are.
  • Your projects, pictures and videos stay in your own browser.
  • We do not use cookies, ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Your Gemini key and GitHub sign-in pass through our server and are never stored.

What stays on your device

Klinth saves your work in your browser's own storage on your computer: your projects, the pictures and videos you upload, and a few small settings (which project you opened last, the name of the GitHub repository you published to, and when you last downloaded a backup). None of this is sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so keep a backup by downloading your project file.

If you choose to remember your Gemini key, it is stored in your browser encrypted with a passphrase that only you know. We never see or store the passphrase. This protects the key from casual access to your computer. It cannot protect it from harmful software or browser extensions running while the key is unlocked.

When you use the AI helper

When you ask the AI helper for a change, your browser sends your request, the content of that one section and your Gemini key to Klinth's server. The server adds its own writing instructions and passes everything to Google's Gemini service, using your key, then sends the answer back to you. Our server does not store your key, your request or the answer, and does not write them to any log.

Google receives this content under your own Gemini account, and its terms and privacy policy apply to it. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products, so do not put private information into AI requests.

When you publish to GitHub

When you connect GitHub, our server only swaps the one-time code from GitHub for a sign-in token and hands it straight to your browser. The token is not stored or logged on our side, and your browser keeps it only in memory: it is gone when you close or reload the tab. Your browser then sends your site files directly to GitHub. GitHub's terms and privacy policy apply to your repository and any GitHub Pages site.

What our server sees

Klinth's website and server run on Cloudflare. Like any website, they receive your IP address and basic browser details when you connect. To stop abuse of the AI helper, the server counts AI requests per IP address. That count is deleted automatically once your limit has refilled, within about an hour. Cloudflare may process connection data to keep the service secure, under its own privacy policy. We keep no other records about you.

Sites you make

Sites you export carry no Klinth badge, tracking or link back to us. If you add custom code, videos from YouTube or Vimeo, or a contact form service, those services may collect data from your visitors. You are responsible for your own site's privacy notice.

Children

Klinth is not aimed at children. If you are under the age where your country requires a parent's permission to use online services, ask a parent or guardian first.

Your choices

  • Delete a project from the start screen, or clear Klinth's data in your browser.
  • Use "Forget my key" in the AI helper to remove a saved Gemini key.
  • Use "Disconnect" in the publish window, or revoke Klinth in your GitHub settings.

Because we keep no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
